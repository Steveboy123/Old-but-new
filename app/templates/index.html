RECOVERY-AWARE RISK CONTROL

Smart Loss Guard

Starts a recovery watch after a soft drawdown instead of instantly selling. It can reset if the trade recovers, rescue a recovered trade near break-even, or exit on a hard cap / confirmed adverse movement. No loss rule can know whether a closed trade would have recovered later.

Disconnect protection: Smart Loss is local logic and cannot react while your PC is offline. Use the Emergency server loss cap above if you want a Deriv-side backstop during disconnects.
