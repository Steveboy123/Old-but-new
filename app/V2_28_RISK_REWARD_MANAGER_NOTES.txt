Dr Exploit Control Nexus V2.28 — Risk:Reward Manager

NEW: RISK:REWARD MANAGER (Multiplier engine, Session setup page)
- One-click ratios: 1:1, 1:1.5, 1:2, 1:3, 1:4, 1:5, or any custom 1:0.25 – 1:20.
- Choose how 1R (the most one trade may lose) is measured:
    Fixed amount per trade ($)
    % of the stake per split (follows compounding)
    % of session capital (classic "risk 1% per trade")
    Step ticks against the trade (price-distance bracket)
- Every new trade gets its own stop loss (1R) and take profit (ratio x R),
  calculated from its real stake when the order is sent. No manual TP/SL maths.
- Money brackets are attached to the Deriv order (limit_order stop_loss and
  take_profit), so they keep working if this PC disconnects.
- Tick brackets are watched locally tick by tick; Deriv-side backups are placed
  1.5x further away as a disconnect safety net.
- Open trades keep the bracket they opened with. Changing the ratio live only
  affects new trades.

HOW IT WORKS WITH THE EXISTING EXITS
- Fixed / Adaptive: TP = the ratio target. The adaptive range is replaced.
- Smart / Hybrid: Deriv closes at the full target; trailing protection starts
  at the profit-lock trigger (or +1R when the lock is off) and never protects
  less than the locked profit. Giveback %, min distance and Hybrid reversal
  rules still apply.
- The Emergency server loss cap is replaced by the 1R stop for new trades.
- Smart Loss Guard still works; the panel warns if its hard cap is tighter
  than 1R.

PROFIT LOCK (optional)
- Arm at +1R / +1.5R / +2R (or any value below the target).
- Once armed, the trade is closed if it falls back to the kept profit
  (default +0.10R, roughly break-even after commission).

SESSION LIMITS IN R (optional)
- Stop new entries at -X R or at +X R for the session (e.g. -3R / +6R).
- Works alongside the existing money profit target / loss limit.

LIVE CALCULATOR (no number crunching)
- Shows the next trade's SL and TP in money and estimated Step ticks,
  risk as % of stake, break-even win rate, max open risk (max open x 1R),
  and expectancy at your current history win rate.
- Ratio comparison table for 1:1 – 1:5: target, win rate needed, expectancy.
  Tap a row to use it.
- Plain-language notes explain exactly what the bot will do with the setup.

R-MULTIPLE TRACKING
- trades_v2.csv gains rr_enabled, rr_ratio, risk_amount, reward_amount,
  risk_ticks, reward_ticks, stop_loss, rr_lock_armed, r_multiple and session_r.
  Older history files are upgraded in place once (old rows keep their values;
  the new columns are blank).
- Multiplier history shows Total R, Average R per trade, R win rate and
  best/worst R. The console logs each trade's R and the running session R.
- Advanced dashboard positions show each trade's SL/TP bracket and live R
  (🔒 = profit lock armed).

ADVANCED DASHBOARD
- Protection tab: Risk:Reward on/off, ratio, basis, risk value, lock, keep —
  "Apply Risk:Reward live" changes the bracket for new trades without a restart.

MULTIPLIER LAB
- Every tester can use its own Risk:Reward ratio and risk per trade.
- "+ Ratio test 1:1–1:4" adds four DEMO testers with the same strategy and
  stake that differ only in ratio, so you can measure which ratio pays.
- Fixed: Multiplier Lab no longer counts Rise/Fall Lab tester cards as its own
  (previously "Run comparison" could start extra unintended EMA/RSI testers
  and the 8-tester limit was reached early).

SAFETY / COMPATIBILITY
- Off by default. Existing presets and sessions behave exactly as before.
- AI Trade forces the manager off so it runs only exits validated in the Lab.
- Rise/Fall (fixed-payout contracts) is unchanged: Risk:Reward does not apply.
- Higher ratios usually lower the win rate. Test on Demo first. Deriv
  commission is charged on every trade, so very small brackets lose more edge.
  If Deriv rejects a stop as too small, the console says so; raise the risk
  per trade or the stake.

INSTALL
V2.28 upgrades the existing Control Nexus installation in place and preserves
credentials, presets, history, lab runs and optimizer settings through the
existing installer backup/restore flow.
