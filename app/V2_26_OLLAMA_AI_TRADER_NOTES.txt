Dr Exploit Control Nexus V2.26 — Ollama AI Trader

NEW
- Replaced OpenAI cloud API integration with self-hosted Ollama.

- Test Ollama connection and list installed local/server models.

- New AI Trade mode for both engines.

AI TRADE
- Requires Allow AI Trade = On and typing LIVE every time the mode starts.
- Uses only candidates that were already validated by the AI Lab and meet the
  configured live minimum trades / profit-factor / edge thresholds.
- The deterministic local trading bot still decides and executes entries. Ollama
  selects among validated configurations; it does not get a raw buy/sell API.
- AI cannot change stake, capital, max-open count or trades-per-signal.
- Compounding is forced off when AI Trade starts.
- Configurable session maximum loss, profit target and maximum trade count.
- Native bot profit/loss caps are also populated for redundant stopping.
- Default fail-closed behavior stops the trading engine if Ollama fails during a
  scheduled live adaptation. This may be changed to hold the current validated config.

RESEARCH
- Rise/Fall Auto Lab remains DEMO-only and hard-locked to 2 ticks.
- Multiplier Auto Lab remains DEMO-only.
- Adaptive remains DEMO-only.
- AI Trade may operate DEMO or LIVE depending on the configured Deriv account.

PRIVACY

- No OpenAI key is preserved during V2.26 upgrade.
